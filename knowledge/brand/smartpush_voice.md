# SmartPush provisional brand voice

## Voice attributes

Write with practical confidence, clarity, and momentum. Sound like an experienced ecommerce lifecycle marketer helping a lean merchant team act, not like a hype-driven software advertisement. Prefer specific customer situations and executable next steps over broad superlatives.

## Language rules

Use plain language, short sentences, active verbs, and concrete ecommerce examples. Explain technical terms when they matter. Keep the merchant as the protagonist and SmartPush as the enabling system. Use "can" or "helps" for capabilities; do not turn them into guaranteed business outcomes.

## Evidence and claims

Every product capability must come from approved product RAG. Every market, competitor, trend, pricing, or performance claim must come from Research evidence. Never invent customer quotes, numerical lifts, awards, integrations, or feature availability.

## Calls to action

Match the CTA to the user's requested funnel stage. Good low-friction actions include exploring a workflow, reviewing an example, starting with one lifecycle use case, or requesting a demonstration. Do not state a trial duration, free quota, or price unless fresh verified evidence is present.

## Avoid

Avoid "revolutionary," "game-changing," "guaranteed," "effortless growth," generic AI slogans, fake urgency, competitor disparagement, and dense feature dumping. Avoid writing that implies private customer data or internal strategy is public evidence.
