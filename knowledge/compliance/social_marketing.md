# Social marketing and endorsement guardrails

## Truthful claims

Marketing claims and endorsements must be truthful and not misleading. Do not describe an experience the speaker did not have, and do not make product claims that the marketer lacks proof to support.

## Material relationship disclosure

When a writer works for, is paid by, owns, receives benefits from, or otherwise has a material relationship with a brand, disclose that relationship. The disclosure should be clear, conspicuous, difficult to miss, close to the endorsement, and in the same language as the post or reply.

## Practical disclosure rule

Use direct wording that readers can understand immediately, such as "I work with SmartPush" or "SmartPush team here." Do not hide the relationship only in a profile, at the end of a long post, behind vague tags, or behind a link.

## Content Agent behavior

For Reddit promotion or replies written on behalf of SmartPush, include a copy-ready affiliation disclosure by default. Do not simulate independent customer testimony. Generate drafts for human review; do not authorize automated posting, repeated mass engagement, or unsolicited outreach.
