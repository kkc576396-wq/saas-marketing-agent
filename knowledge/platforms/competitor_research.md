# Competitor research article template

## Scope first

Define the market segment, target customer, geography, comparison date, named competitors, and decision the report should support. Separate direct competitors from adjacent alternatives. Never silently expand the scope.

## Evidence hierarchy

Use current official product, pricing, help-center, integration, and release pages for vendor capabilities. Use independent analysis for market context. Use community posts only as scoped user observations. Record source URL and date for every material claim, and label unknown or unverified fields instead of guessing.

## Comparison dimensions

Select dimensions relevant to the user's decision, such as target ICP, supported channels, campaign creation, segmentation, automation, ecommerce data integration, AI assistance, deliverability controls, analytics, onboarding, pricing model, and service. Do not force every report to use every dimension.

## Required report structure

Return: executive summary; scope and method; competitor profiles; evidence-backed comparison table; strengths by use case; gaps and risks; implications for SmartPush; content or positioning opportunities; evidence limitations; and linked sources with retrieval dates.

## Fairness and claim rules

Use neutral language. Do not call a competitor inferior without evidence. Distinguish missing evidence from a missing feature. Treat price and product availability as time-sensitive. Do not use confidential internal competitor pricing or roadmap information as public evidence.
