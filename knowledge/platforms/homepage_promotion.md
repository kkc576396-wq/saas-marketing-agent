# Homepage promotion writing template

## Required narrative sequence

1. Lead with one recognizable merchant problem or desired outcome.
2. State the product category and value proposition in one plain sentence.
3. Present three to five benefit-led sections, each connecting a merchant job to an approved capability.
4. Show how the workflow operates using a short sequence or scenario.
5. Address the most important trust or adoption friction without overclaiming.
6. End with one primary CTA appropriate to the user's funnel goal.

## Section construction

Each section should contain a benefit-oriented heading, a concise explanation, and optional proof only when verified evidence is available. Keep feature lists subordinate to the merchant outcome. Screenshots or UI callouts may be suggested, but do not pretend an image exists when none was supplied.

## Output contract

Unless the user requests another format, return: page goal, target audience, hero eyebrow, headline, subheadline, primary CTA, optional secondary CTA, benefit sections, workflow/how-it-works section, trust section, FAQ suggestions, and final CTA. Copy should be skimmable and ready for design handoff.

## Guardrails

Do not publish unverified numerical outcomes, outdated prices, plan quotas, guaranteed deliverability, or fabricated customer proof. Do not mix multiple unrelated ICPs in one page. Preserve the language and locale requested by the user.
