# SmartPush approved product knowledge

## Product category and channels

SHOPLINE SmartPush is a marketing automation product for ecommerce merchants. It supports email, SMS, and pop-up touchpoints and can coordinate automated nurture flows across one or more of those channels.

## Customer data and segmentation

SmartPush integrates with SHOPLINE customer data. Merchants can synchronize contacts, import or export contacts, create customer segments, and target or exclude groups when configuring campaigns. Official product material also describes dynamic segmentation using real-time first-party data.

## Campaign and email creation

Merchants can configure recipients, subject lines, preview text, sender details, content, language, scheduling, and UTM tracking. SmartPush provides an AI-powered editor, recommended subject lines, a drag-and-drop email builder, product and discount blocks, preview and test functions, and a library of professionally designed templates.

## Lifecycle automation

SmartPush supports automated messages at customer-journey moments such as subscription invitations, abandoned-cart reminders, order notifications, and post-purchase engagement. Workflows can use customer behavior and lifecycle stages, delays, conditions, and preset templates.

## AI and personalization

Official product material describes AI-powered content creation, send-time recommendations, intent- and interest-based product recommendations, and automatic resend optimization for non-openers.

## Pop-ups and lead capture

SmartPush supports pop-up forms for collecting contact information and for engaging visitors who may otherwise leave without converting.
