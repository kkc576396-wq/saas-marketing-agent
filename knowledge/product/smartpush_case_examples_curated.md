# Curated scenarios from supplied marketing examples

## Segmentation and behavior-triggered ideas

Useful campaign scenarios include segmenting audiences with customer behavior, following up after product views without purchase, reminding customers about expiring offers, and creating different journeys after positive or negative product feedback. These are concept prompts, not proof that every integration or trigger is currently available.

## Abandoned checkout campaign ideas

A useful abandoned-checkout narrative connects a clear merchant pain point to a practical recovery sequence. Potential touchpoints include email, SMS, and pop-ups; useful message elements include product context, a concise reason to return, a relevant offer when appropriate, urgency that is truthful, and one clear call to action.

## AI-assisted workflow ideas

The supplied examples position AI as an assistant for drafting variants, preserving product selling points, recommending products, and helping teams prepare campaigns faster. Public copy must use only the AI capabilities approved in the official product knowledge or freshly verified by Research.

## Seasonal readiness framework

For high-volume promotional periods, a reusable preparation sequence is: capture permissioned leads, define audience segments, prepare browse/cart/order lifecycle automations, coordinate email and SMS, create message variants, test links and rendering, and confirm tracking before launch.

## Excluded claims

Do not reuse conversion-rate claims, GMV lifts, open/click improvements, recovery percentages, customer counts, free-credit amounts, trial lengths, pricing, or quota statements from the four examples. They are unverified or time-sensitive.
